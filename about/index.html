About this app

This is a real process at Mister O1, a pizza restaurant in Boca Raton: staff send every vendor invoice to accounting. This app reads the invoice with Claude, sorts it into the accounting categories, checks the math and duplicates, logs it in the right weekly spreadsheet tab, and emails the summary.

How to test it

  1. Download a sample invoice below.
  2. Sign in with Google.
  3. Upload it and click Read invoice.
  4. Review the categories and click Send.
  5. Check your email and the live spreadsheet.

Try sending the same invoice twice to see the duplicate protection.

Each account can read up to 10 invoices per day. You only ever see your own invoices.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. Upload an invoice: one PDF, or photos of each page taken with your phone (the app combines them into one PDF).
  3. AI reads it: Anthropic's Claude reads the vendor, invoice number, date, total and the category breakdown.
  4. Rules sort it: each vendor has a rule (for example, a dairy supplier always goes to "Dairy"; Gordon Food Service is split using its category summary page).
  5. You check it: you can edit everything. Sending stays locked until the categories add up to the invoice total to the cent, and the app warns you if the invoice was already sent.
  6. Send: the invoice is logged in the right weekly tab of the class demo spreadsheet, saved, and emailed only to you, with the PDF attached.

Built with

Netlify (hosting and server functions), Supabase (sign-in, database, file storage), Anthropic Claude API (reading invoices), Google Sheets API (weekly log), Gmail (emails).
