Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Mister O1 Invoices ("the app") is a student class project by Nicolas Munoz for ISM4421 at Florida Atlantic University. This page explains what the app stores, why, and who else handles it.

What we store

  • Your Google name and email address, received when you sign in with Google, plus the initials you choose (written in the "Sent By" column of the log). Google may also share your profile picture link; the app does not use it.
  • Invoice PDFs you upload (including photos you take, which are combined into a PDF).
  • Invoice data: vendor, invoice number, date, total, category amounts, your comments, which week it belongs to, who sent it, and when.

The app does not ask Google for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account. It only uses Google to confirm who you are.

How it is used

  • To read your invoice with AI, sort it into categories, and show it to you for review.
  • To log it in a test Google spreadsheet owned by the developer and email it to your own email address.
  • To warn about invoices that were already sent, and to show who sent each invoice.

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for anything other than running this app.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and private file storage.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and the server code.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): receives the invoice PDF to read it.
  • Google: sign-in, the test spreadsheet (Google Sheets) and sending the email (Gmail).

Who can see it

Uploaded PDFs are stored privately and are not public. Anyone signed in to the app can see the list of invoices, who sent them, and open their PDFs (the app is built for a small team).

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Data is kept for the duration of the class project. To ask for your account, profile and invoices to be deleted, email 04munoznicolas@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
